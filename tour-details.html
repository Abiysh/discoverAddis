Full Day Private Tour Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
The Experience

Discover Addis through local eyes.

Explore the fascinating capital of Ethiopia on a carefully designed journey through its history, culture, food and everyday life. This private experience combines the city's most important landmarks with authentic local moments.

Your Day

Tour itinerary

01

Morning — City Introduction

Meet your local guide and begin your Addis Ababa adventure. Learn about the history and development of Ethiopia's capital while exploring the city's central areas.

02

Midday — Culture & Heritage

Visit important cultural and historical sites and discover stories that shaped Ethiopia. Your guide will explain the traditions and history behind each place.

03

Afternoon — Local Life

Experience the rhythm of Addis Ababa, explore local neighborhoods and markets, and enjoy an authentic Ethiopian coffee experience.

04

Evening — Farewell

Finish the experience with panoramic views and time to ask your guide questions about Ethiopia, travel and local life.
