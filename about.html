Who We Are

Travel Ethiopia with people who know it.

Discover Addis Tour is a locally focused travel company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Our goal is simple: create memorable experiences that allow visitors to see Ethiopia from a local perspective.

From the busy streets of Addis Ababa to ancient heritage sites, dramatic mountains and traditional coffee ceremonies, we connect travelers with the places and people that make Ethiopia special.

We believe the best travel experiences are not only about taking photographs. They are about meeting people, understanding cultures, tasting local food, hearing stories and creating memories that stay with you long after you leave.

— Discover Ethiopia with us.
